SKUs at HQ

—

Cover = stock on hand ÷ average units sent out per week (IBTs and sales orders) over the last 8 weeks of data. Production components are left out. The date filter doesn't apply here; the product filters do.

ABC–XYZ: value and predictability

A/B/C = share of outbound value over the last 26 weeks (A = top 80%, B = next 15%, C = the rest). X/Y/Z = how steady weekly demand is (X steady, Y variable, Z erratic). AX items need the tightest stock control; CZ items are candidates to cut. Click a cell to see the SKUs.
